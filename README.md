## 💡 Overview## 🛑 The Problem
Family members which can't be in constant touch with their elderly parents or close elderly relatives due to their work, struggles to monitor their health , instant check ups, and wellbeing.

## 🎯 Our Solution
An interactive Web App which voice call elderly members and ask regular health and wellness related question and deliver the overall status to their Caregiver  
------------------------------
## 🧰 Tech Stack


* Frontend: TypeScript with SvelteKit (Static Adapter) and UnoCSS
* Backend:  Python with FastAPI
* Database & Storage: Supabase 
* Deployment & Cloud: Vercel,Bland AI & Twilio

## 🔮 Future   Integrations

* Background Monitoring: Continuous voice environment scanning that runs quietly in the background without needing manual activation.
* Voice Recognition System: Advanced biometric matching to verify the senior's unique voice and prevent accidental triggers from television background noise or visitors.
* Emergency Callouts: Hands-free voice trigger phrases (e.g., "Help, Vcare!") that allow seniors to call out to the AI instantly during accidents or falls. [1] 
* Instant Caregiver Alerts: Immediate, zero-latency emergency event routing straight to the caregiver mobile application and web dashboard. [2, 3] 
* Frictionless Conversation: Enhanced natural language tracking to make interactions feel significantly smoother, allowing for interruptions or pauses without breaking the system loop. [4]
